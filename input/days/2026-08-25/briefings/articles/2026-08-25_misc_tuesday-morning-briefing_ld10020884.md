## Germany's first weight-loss pill available from September

![](https://img-msp-prod.nzz.ch/2026/08/14/ffb92c54-b355-480c-93d1-9531e2fd3625.jpg?width=1024)
** (Hannah Beier / Reuters)

**What happened:** Anyone in Germany who wants to take a weight-loss drug can take one tablet daily starting in September. It contains the same active ingredient as the weekly Ozempic or Wegovy injections. Like these, the preparation is approved for overweight or obese patients who already have a weight-related comorbidity. The tablet is already prescribed as a treatment for type 2 diabetes. Read the report

**Why it matters: **Health insurance funds cover costs only for diabetics. Many patients lose 10 to 15 kilograms of body weight with injections under real-world conditions, but only about half continue a year after starting therapy. Those who do not continue permanently regain the lost kilograms. This is unlikely to change significantly with the tablets. Side effects primarily include gastrointestinal issues such as nausea, diarrhea, vomiting or constipation.

## London reaffirms support on Ukraine's Independence Day

**What happened:** Ukraine marked the 35th anniversary of its independence yesterday. In his address, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy emphasized his country's will to resist: «Ukraine wants peace but is not ready to simply surrender.» He called on partners for more support, especially for air defense. The most important pledge of the day concerned offensive weapons. Great Britain gave the green light for the transfer of crucial armaments technology. Read the report

**Why it matters:** Russia strongly condemned the fact that the Storm Shadow/Scalp cruise missile, based on British and French technology, can soon be produced in Ukraine. The guided missile has a range of 250 kilometers. «Great Britain is part of the war and regularly pours oil on the fire,» a Kremlin spokesperson said. Germany still opposes the delivery of the comparable Taurus cruise missile to Ukraine.

**Background: **After the official celebrations, the so-called Coalition of the Willing held a hybrid meeting. The meeting was chaired by British Prime Minister Andy Burnham, who made his first foreign trip since taking office in Kyiv. Chancellor Friedrich Merz, like French President Emmanuel Macron and many others, participated via video. The U.S. was not represented. Follow the live ticker

**In the economic war against Iran, the U.S. government is now suddenly relying on «quiet diplomacy»:** U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is refraining for now from imposing sanctions on Iran's trading partners. He wants to give the affected states one last chance – after previously threatening to bring Tehran to its knees. Read the report

**U.S. Supreme Court temporarily allows Trump's mail-in ballot plans:** Just over two months before the American parliamentary elections, the Supreme Court on Monday lifted a temporary blockade of President Donald Trump's plans to tighten mail-in voting rules. Although a final decision on the matter is still pending, the administration can at least provisionally continue to pursue its plans. Read the latest developments

**Canada breaks off customs negotiations with the U.S.:** The neighboring country left the negotiating table and suspended ongoing negotiations for a customs agreement. U.S. President Trump then announced on Monday that he would increase punitive tariffs on cars, auto parts and steel from Canada. Read the report
