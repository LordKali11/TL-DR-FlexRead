_(dpa)/ehj_. According to initial findings, fires in the U.S. state of Nevada destroyed 32 homes and damaged six more. The fire department reported these figures were likely to increase. Emergency services said progress was being made after wildfires had triggered a major operation in the state. The so-called «Hawk Fire» in a hilly region on the outskirts of Reno was 27% contained, emergency services announced Monday.

The flames spread rapidly since Saturday. Most recently, the fires affected an area of more than 6,000 hectares. Authorities said seven people, including three emergency personnel, were injured. The severe fires temporarily prompted 42,000 people to evacuate, and another 45,000 were told to prepare to leave their homes if necessary. By Monday, only 23,000 residents remained under evacuation orders.

Emergency services explained the fire had been caused by humans, without providing details. Investigations are ongoing.
