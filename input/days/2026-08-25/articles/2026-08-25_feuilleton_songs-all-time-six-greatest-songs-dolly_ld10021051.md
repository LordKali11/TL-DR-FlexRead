# Songs for all time: The six greatest songs by Dolly Parton

*Poverty, pain, and love: The country icon with the blonde wigs and the bright soprano voice has fallen silent. Millions see themselves in her songs and her story. What are they telling us?*

Country legend Dolly Parton died at the age of 80 in Nashville. Born on January 19, 1946, one of twelve children in a poor Tennessee family, she wrote her first songs as a child and became a member of the country radio show «Grand Ole Opry» at age 13.

In a career spanning over six decades, she wrote an estimated 3,000 songs, including several global hits. A selection.

## «My Tennessee Mountain Home»

> [embed: youtube ]

_ael._ «My Tennessee Mountain Home» is Dolly Parton's homage to her childhood in the Smoky Mountains. The song describes the simple life Parton knew as one of twelve children in Locust Ridge, Tennessee: sitting on the porch, watching siblings play with May beetles, chasing fireflies, listening to crickets.

Parton wrote the song herself and released it as a single in 1972; a year later, it became the title track of her 1973 concept album. The entire album is a musical autobiography. The album cover shows the actual log cabin where Dolly Parton grew up. Parton later bought back and restored the house. The song later became the theme song for her Dollywood amusement park.

## «Jolene»

> [embed: youtube ]

_inn_. She pleads, she begs, yet she is so powerful. «Jolene, Jolene,» Dolly Parton sings — for the first time in 1973 — «don’t take my man.» With this, she ultimately addresses everyone's fear of being replaceable. «Jolene» is this beauty, with «auburn hair,» «ivory skin,» and «emerald eyes.» She could have practically anyone. But the man Dolly fears losing, she should leave him alone, leave him to her.

A typical jealousy story? No.

The country singer does not condemn «Jolene»; she portrays her as someone admirable. It is not aggression; it is simply her own fear of loss that she addresses. It is her anxiety, and with it, exactly what Dolly Parton spoke to for many years. Or rather, from the gut, because the pain of being abandoned sits there.

## «Here You Come Again»

> [embed: youtube ]

_ael._ «Here You Come Again, just when I’ve begun to get myself together»: Dolly Parton's first major pop crossover hit from 1977 is about a woman who is just getting over a past relationship when her charming, confident ex-partner appears.

Unlike almost all her other hits, Dolly Parton did not write it herself. It comes from the songwriting couple Barry Mann and Cynthia Weil. The famous opening line «Here You Come Again» came about by accident: Cynthia Weil accidentally deleted part of the original lyric and had to improvise. Her husband liked her new version better than the original.

The song marked the beginning of Dolly Parton's worldwide mainstream success. At that time, Parton was already an established country star. However, she was afraid of losing her core audience with a too «slick» pop song. To make her country roots more audible, she included a steel guitar. The song reached number one on the country charts and number three on the pop charts.

## «9 to 5»

> [embed: youtube ]

_mij_. First, this hard piano beat, then the trumpet comes in – and Dolly's voice. The singer stumbles out of bed and pours herself a high-proof «cup of ambition.» She gets ready for «9 to 5,» the job, the daily madness, the disappointments, and the small triumphs. «Out on the street, the traffic starts jumping,» she sings, and in the video, businesswomen rush through the big city to the beat of a metronome. Dolly sits with her band in the barn, more country yet «all business»: red blouse, blue jeans. And that incredible hair.

«9 to 5» is also the title song for the Hollywood blockbuster of the same name. The story about three women who kidnap their boss because he spreads lies about them grossed 100 million dollars. Parton always has a clear message. But she never allows herself to be co-opted politically. Dolly is program enough. That's why Americans love her so much.

## «Islands In The Stream» with Kenny Rogers

> [embed: youtube ]

_inn_. No, no, it is not «Ghetto Supastar,» not the earworm by American rapper Pras Michel. Nor is it the Bee Gees, who had a comeback with «Islands In The Stream» in 1997. It is Dolly Parton and her country colleague Kenny Rogers, who in 1983 happily moved through country clubs with the song, stood on arena stages with it, and performed on television.

While Dolly Parton still sings about the vulnerable side of love in «Jolene,» «Islands In The Stream» is a kind of anthem to unconditional connection. The two celebrate the intimacy between two people; the adversities of the world should not harm them.

Rogers actually wanted to record the song alone in a Nashville studio, but found it stiff and boring. Coincidence or not, Parton was also in town at the time and came at the perfect time. The duet song became a hit. Not just for the two of them.

The Bee Gees actually wrote it — and reclaimed it fourteen years after Dolly's and Kenny's triumph.

## «I Will Always Love You»

> [embed: youtube ]

_mij._ Everyone knows Whitney Houston's beautiful version. Some still see the singer in the arms of bodyguard Kevin Costner. But the heart is truly touched only when Dolly sings the sad love ballad. It is about separation and starting a new chapter: «I’m not what you need,» she sang, separating from her mentor and manager Porter Wagoner in 1974. Dolly took control of her career, asserting herself as a woman in the tough country business. And she paid a price for it. But Dolly holds no grudge. «I wish you joy and happiness,» she sang. Dolly's voice almost breaks with sadness, Whitney Houston calls out the words bravely into the night. Now both voices have fallen silent.
