When Nvidia publishes financial figures, the world holds its breath. They are crucial for investors' confidence in the ongoing AI boom. If the figures disappoint on Wednesday, this could trigger a correction or even a stock market crash. The AI boom in its current form would be jeopardized.

Companies playing a role in the AI revolution can now be found across various industries. Besides language model developers like OpenAI, hyperscalers like Amazon, and chip providers, it also includes energy companies and technology suppliers.

Nvidia, however, remains the most important «shovel manufacturer.» Without its high-performance chips, the expansion of a powerful AI infrastructure is inconceivable. Morningstar analysts, for example, see «no risk whatsoever to Nvidia's competitive advantage.»

## Markets at risk of crashing

Should Nvidia, however, indicate that sales momentum slowed in the second quarter, this could send the stock market tumbling. So far, Nvidia has always managed to surprise the doubters. The company has exceeded profit forecasts in each of the last 14 quarters since the beginning of the AI boom in late 2022. And it has consolidated its position as the most valuable company. Nvidia has a market capitalization of around 5 trillion dollars.

Observers also assume that Nvidia will exceed expectations for the second quarter. To achieve this, Nvidia would have to double its quarterly revenue and achieve almost 100% profit growth for the year. Regardless, it is clear: the results will be followed by price fluctuations that will sway the entire market. Investors tend to sell Nvidia shares even after record profits and secure their gains.

The shares are no longer on a self-sustaining upward trend. After years of strong price gains, Nvidia has had to settle for moderate advances this year. Broad-based technology indices such as the Nasdaq or the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index, which tracks the chip industry, have also been moving sideways for months. Fluctuations and regular price drops attest to investor nervousness. There have also been initial casualties, such as the collapse of the hedge fund Situational Awareness, which specializes in AI stocks, in July.

## Unshakeable optimism

Jensen Huang, co-founder and CEO of Nvidia, asserts that the AI revolution is only just beginning. He spreads optimism at every opportunity. Following the strong first-quarter results, he said: «Demand is skyrocketing. The reason for this is simple, agentic AI is here – it is doing productive work, creating real value, and rapidly spreading across companies and industries.»

Huang describes investor unease about circular financing structures as «ridiculous.» Nvidia is at the center of a network of AI companies that buy from and invest in each other. However, Huang says the investments are merely financial guarantees. He calls them a sign of «trust in companies that are shaping an entire generation.»

For a long time, hyperscalers were able to finance AI expansion with their own cash flows. Despite their high financial power, they recently have incurred debt and issued bonds to manage the enormous AI investments. This type of external financing does not in itself pose a problem. The bond market readily accepts the debt securities, and the bonds are highly creditworthy. But Huang wants to ensure that financing capacity does not decrease.

## Nvidia's customer financing

At the beginning of the month, Nvidia teamed up with six financial institutions to launch a financing plan of over 500 billion dollars for AI expansion. Well-known names such as Goldman Sachs, Blackrock, and KKR are participating. If the project is fully implemented, it would be one of the largest private financing initiatives Wall Street has ever undertaken. It involves a series of capital pools through which Nvidia customers can secure financing. Huang views computing power as a new, investable asset class, as a «capital market for AI infrastructure.»

In addition to financing, Nvidia has also advanced into the area of language models. Sources recently revealed that the chip group invested six billion dollars in an open language model from the startup Poolside. In March, Nvidia had already invested in Thinking Machines Labs, also an open AI model. Nvidia is thus increasingly covering the AI value chain and is becoming even more indispensable for the industry. Thus, all AI players have an interest in Nvidia continuing to report strong figures.
