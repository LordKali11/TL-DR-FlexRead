# The Zurich FDP loses its last district school board presidency. Përparim Avdili says: «My incomprehension of the GLP could hardly be greater.»

*The FDP narrowly loses to the SP in the election for the presidency of the Glattal district school board. The SP now leads six of seven district school boards.*

On Sunday, an era ended for the FDP with a defeat in the elections for the presidency of Zurich's District School Board 11. The schools in Oerlikon, Seebach, and Affoltern are now led by an SP district school president. SP candidate Marco Hochuli narrowly won with 3,645 votes against his competitor, FDP candidate Carlina Capaul, who received 3,588 votes. The FDP thus lost its last district school board presidency.

Marco Hochuli, 49, is a trained nurse. He previously worked as head of the housing and homelessness department at the Social Welfare Department. Before his candidacy for president of the Glattal district school board, Hochuli was already a member of the Waidberg district school board.

His opponent, Carlina Capaul, 45, heads central services at a Zurich cantonal school. Capaul was expected to succeed Vera Lang Temperli of the FDP, who most recently held the office of president of the Glattal district school board.

Six of seven district school presidencies are now held by the SP; only the one on the Zürichberg is not. Independent Roger Curchod leads that district school board.

## FDP criticizes GLP

After the election, the FDP criticized the GLP. The Green Liberals had nominated their own candidate, Cantonal Councillor Monica Sanesi, in the first round of voting. However, she withdrew before the second round, and the GLP then decided not to issue an election recommendation.

Përparim Avdili, president of the Zurich City FDP, says: «The GLP's recommendation could have been decisive, and my incomprehension could hardly be greater.» The decision not to support the FDP candidate also showed the Green Liberals' positioning, Avdili says. «The GLP proves once again that it calls itself liberal but actually supports the expansion of the SP's power.»

The GLP defends itself against these accusations – and in turn attacks the FDP. Selina Frey, co-president of the Zurich City GLP, says: «The FDP could also have supported our candidate in the first round of voting, as we are ultimately better at connecting both poles.» An election recommendation for FDP candidate Capaul was out of the question for the GLP, Frey says. «With the free vote, everything was open to our voters, and a recommendation would not have changed anything. The fact that six of seven district school presidencies are now in the hands of the SP is also regrettable for us.»

## Left-wing man wins against conservative woman

Avdili also criticized the role of the GLP and SP because of the candidate's gender: the FDP nominated a woman, the SP a man. Avdili says: «And now, precisely those parties that normally advocate so strongly for equality are supporting the election of a man. That makes no sense.»

The SP counters this criticism. Party President Oliver Heimgartner says: «The district school boards are currently led by six women and one man. Therefore, it is really not a problem that another man was elected.»

And GLP's Frey says: «After Monica Sanesi's withdrawal, we decided on a free vote. With that, we focused on the person and not the gender.»

## Controversy surrounding the board

The administration of Zurich's schools repeatedly leads to discussions, partly because many individuals are involved. The schools are led by the head of the City of Zurich's School and Sports Department, currently Green Party member Balthasar Glättli. The district school boards operate one level below the City Council; each is led by an elected official. This official is employed full-time and earns around 190,000 Swiss francs per year for their mandate. The district school boards then elect school principals, who are responsible for individual schools.

In 2021, a major scandal occurred when SP school president Roberto Rodriguez voluntarily resigned from his position and received severance pay of 650,000 francs, even though he already had a new job. Reforms followed, resulting in district school board presidents no longer being compensated when they leave office.

Responsibility for leading the schools is therefore broadly distributed. This has repeatedly drawn criticism, and the system was meant to be reformed multiple times to increase efficiency. However, those discussions stalled. SP President Heimgartner says the population wants the schools to be well-anchored in the neighborhoods, adding that more centralism would not make the schools better.

FDP President Avdili, however, makes a suggestion. He believes that one could consider electing all district school board presidencies together using the proportional representation system. Avdili says: «That way, the strongest party in the city would not automatically have such a great advantage.» Whether and when this discussion will be held, however, remains open.
