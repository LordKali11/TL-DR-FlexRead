# Former German Foreign Minister Baerbock gets a job at a prestigious US university – and is ridiculed for it

*From the United Nations to the lecture hall: Annalena Baerbock's New York professorship draws criticism*

Annalena Baerbock remains in the city where, by her own admission, she feels so at home: After the end of her term as president of the United Nations General Assembly, the Green Party politician is taking on a teaching position at the renowned Columbia University in New York. The former German Foreign Minister will become a Professor of Global Leadership and Co-Director of the Master of Public Administration in Global Leadership program.

Baerbock's tenure as president of the General Assembly ends on Sept. 8 and was never intended to be a permanent position. The term is always tied to the body's one-year session, and the post is awarded to a different country each year. The German's successor will be the experienced diplomat Khalilur Rahman, who has been Bangladesh's foreign minister since February.

Baerbock's career move indicates that she is not aiming for a return to active politics, at least for now. In an interview with the German Press Agency shortly after taking office, she spoke about how much she enjoyed «disappearing» into the diversity of New York City, saying it was «liberating» for her.

## Criticism of the university

Public reactions to Baerbock's new role came immediately and were partly derisive. Wolfgang Kubicki, chairman of The Liberals (FDP), commented on the job change on the platform X with the question: «What did Merz know when he declared he did not want his children to study in the U.S.?»

AfD politician Maximilian Krah, who holds a degree from Columbia University's Business School, complained that Baerbock had «no academic background.» He believed his degree was «devalued» by the Green politician's involvement and said he would write to the university to ask for «clarification.»

The focus of the Global Leadership Program is, however, more practical than academic.

The Master of Public Administration in Global Leadership is a ten-month full-time program at the School of International and Public Affairs (Sipa) for experienced executives. The admission requirement is more than ten years of professional experience. According to its own description, the program targets leaders from business, politics, and the non-profit sector. It particularly promotes the exchange of internationally renowned leaders, a group that Baerbock will probably also join in the future.

## Reportedly «chief diplomat of the United Nations»

During Baerbock's time at the United Nations, international crises such as the wars in Ukraine and the Middle East dominated the agenda. For her conduct in office, Baerbock received unusually strong criticism. The United States and Russia accused her last week of overstepping her authority as president of the General Assembly.

The politician also repeatedly drew attention to herself, for example by providing rather uninhibited insights into her New York daily life on social media – from taxi rides to working in the General Assembly building.

In Germany, she caused astonishment in February with an appearance on Sandra Maischberger's talk show: There, she described herself as «the chief diplomat of the United Nations, who speaks on behalf of 193 states.»

This self-description does not correspond to the rather ceremonial duties of the president of the General Assembly; it most closely matches the position of the UN Secretary-General, although by no means all member states feel represented by him. Either way: Baerbock did not hold this position.
