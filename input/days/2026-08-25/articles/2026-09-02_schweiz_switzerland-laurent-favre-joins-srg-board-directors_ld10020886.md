## Laurent Favre becomes new SRG Board of Directors member

_ela_. The Federal Council elected Laurent Favre to the SRG Board of Directors at its meeting on Wednesday (9/2). Favre will take office on April 1, 2027, and will replace Ursula Gut-Winterberger.

Favre is a cantonal government member for the canton of Neuchâtel and has been heading the Department of Spatial Planning and Environment since 2014. From 2020 to 2024, he chaired the Conference of Cantonal Public Transport Directors (KöV), and since 2025, he will chair the Conference of Cantonal Energy Directors (EnDK). He was also president of the Tripartite Conference, the political platform of the Confederation, cantons, cities, and municipalities for promoting cooperation between government levels and between urban and rural areas.

## Bernese court halt to voting newspaper for SVP neutrality initiative

_jsc._ A Bernese civil court classified a passage in the French-language voting newspaper of the Yes committee for the SVP neutrality initiative as unlawful. «20 Minuten» reported this on Wednesday (9/2), citing the ruling. The newspaper contains the message that «the Left and the Greens» say yes to the initiative: «La gauche et les Verts disent oui.»

The Greens, who rejected the neutrality initiative, challenged the passage before a Bernese civil court because the party's headquarters are located there. The court had provisionally decided that the French-language version had to be adjusted online immediately. The party confirmed the information upon inquiry from the NZZ. The reason is likely that «Les Verts» corresponds exactly to the party name of the Greens in French-speaking Switzerland, while «Left and Greens» can also be understood as a designation of an ideological current.

The initiators adjusted the online version of the newspaper shortly after the publication of the «20 Minuten» report, so that the Left and the Greens no longer appeared in the passage.

## Ticino cantonal government member Claudio Zali investigated for alleged sexual offenses

_ela_. The Ticino prosecutor's office opened a new criminal proceeding against Lega-cantonal government member Claudio Zali. As RSI reported on Tuesday (9/1), the case concerned alleged offenses against sexual integrity that are said to have occurred several years ago. The broadcaster said the proceedings were triggered by statements from a third person. According to RSI, Zali had already been questioned as an accused person and denied all criminal responsibility.

According to RSI, the new investigations were also connected to a 52-year-old woman who was questioned as a witness in July and later found dead in Lake Lugano. A separate proceeding is already underway against Zali for abuse of office and attempted coercion in connection with the detention of a 14-year-old in La Farera prison. Due to these accusations, among others, Zali intended to resign as Ticino cantonal government member by the end of September. The Ticino cantonal government urged him on Tuesday to resign immediately; Zali had not complied by the evening. The presumption of innocence applies to him.

## Federal Councillor Beat Jans's meeting with German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt

_lia._ Federal Councillor Beat Jans met with German Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt in Chiasso on Monday (8/31). As the Federal Department of Justice and Police announced, the two exchanged views on border security issues and cross-border police cooperation and explored how cooperation in these areas could be deepened between the countries.

It will also be examined whether a police cooperation center at the German-Swiss border or the expansion of the existing liaison office in Basel could improve the exchange of information between security authorities in Switzerland and Germany.

## Confederation's introduction of biometric identity card

_ela_. The Federal Council adopted the legal basis for this on Wednesday (6/26), as it wrote in a statement. From November 2, Swiss citizens will be able to apply for the card, which is equipped with a chip, a facial image, and two fingerprints. It is intended especially for travel to the EU; the previous identity card without a chip remains available. Already issued non-biometric cards remain valid until their expiration. The fees are not changing.

## Federal Council acceleration of Cell Broadcast introduction

_ela_. The Federal Council intends to warn the population more quickly via mobile phone about dangers in the future, as it wrote in a statement on Wednesday (8/26). For the introduction of Cell Broadcast, it requested a commitment credit of 15.8 million Swiss francs from parliament. The system sends warning messages to all mobile phones in an affected area and is intended to supplement sirens, radio, and Alertswiss. The total costs for setup and operation until 2035 are estimated at 67.7 million francs.

## Continuing decrease in asylum applications in Switzerland

_inn._ The State Secretariat for Migration (SEM) expected significantly fewer asylum seekers this year than originally assumed. The Confederation lowered its forecast from previously around 26,000 to an estimated 22,000 applications. It announced this on Tuesday (8/25) in its fact sheet, stating there were two reasons for this: On the one hand, the number of crossings on the central Mediterranean route had decreased, and on the other hand, fewer refugees and migrants had been traveling on the Balkan route this year than in the previous year. The SEM recorded the largest decrease in applications from Afghanistan, Turkey, and Eritrea in the first half of the year.

## Glacier collapse threat in Valais municipality of Randa

_cov_. The Valais municipality of Randa increased monitoring of the Weisshorn-Ost hanging glacier above the village on Monday (8/24). As the authorities announced on the municipality's website, it had been determined that the ice masses were moving faster and could lead to a collapse. The glacier is now being observed at shorter intervals.

The municipality defined a danger zone that may not be entered until further notice. The authorities warned that in the coming days, collapses with a possible reach into the marked danger zone were possible. According to the map, only individual buildings are located within it. The majority of the village is located outside, but very close to the endangered area. They added that the applicable cordons and instructions must be strictly observed.
