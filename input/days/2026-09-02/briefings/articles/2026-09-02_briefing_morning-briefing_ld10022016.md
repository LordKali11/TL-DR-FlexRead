## The Most Important News This Morning

## German accusation against Russia in Leipzig drone attack

![](https://img-msp-prod.nzz.ch/2026/09/01/417bf6d8-3ea8-4a68-881b-ddea5e70d04e.jpg?width=1024)
** (Sean Gallup / Getty)

**What happened:** The Federal Government for the first time blamed Russia for the attempted bomb attack on Leipzig Airport a month ago. Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt explained that intelligence findings clearly pointed to «Russian involvement.» The modus operandi and tools used were known from other Russian operations, he added. Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul presented a package of measures against Russia. To the report

**Why it matters:** According to Mr. Wadephul, the Russian Consulate General in Bonn would close in September. In addition, the agreement for the Russian House in Berlin, which is suspected of being too close to espionage activities, would be terminated. New individuals would be proposed for the EU sanctions list, entry controls for Russian nationals would be tightened, and pressure on the Russian shadow fleet in the Baltic Sea would be increased.

**How Russia is reacting:** President Vladimir Putin called the punitive measures a «grave mistake.» «Where is the evidence?» he asked at a press conference. Evidence presented so far was «fabricated,» he said. The German reaction was primarily politically motivated domestically, as Chancellor Friedrich Merz had lost the trust of the citizens, Putin added. To the live ticker

**Our assessment:** The overall view of the many hybrid actions against supporters of Ukraine allows no other conclusion than that Russia is behind the Leipzig attack. Now, the Federal Government sets boundaries but does not overreact. That is smart. An overreaction would only have unnecessarily fueled Russia apologists from the AfD and BSW. To the commentary

## US and Iran: new mutual attacks

**What happened:** The Iran War experienced the heaviest fighting in weeks. The U.S. initially targeted sites near the Strait of Hormuz yesterday. Iran's Revolutionary Guard announced the start of a retaliatory action against American bases shortly thereafter. Jordan and Kuwait then reported Iranian missile and drone attacks. To the live ticker

**The background:** Barron Trump, the 20-year-old son of the U.S. President, has become a target of the Iranian regime. Under the title «Where Barron Trump is to be killed,» state media published an AI-generated video that offered a bounty of millions. Vienna-based Iran expert Walter Posch interprets the video as a sign of nervousness. «The Iranians want to make it clear once again: We are very strong, and we can do this,» he said. For background

**Why it matters: **There were also reportedly assassination plots in the US against a son of Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. Iran was likely primarily concerned with creating uncertainty and psychological pressure. The message was that even the closest circles of the world's best-protected politicians were reachable. At the same time, the regime demonstrated its capacity to act to its own population – at a time when it was fighting for its own survival.
