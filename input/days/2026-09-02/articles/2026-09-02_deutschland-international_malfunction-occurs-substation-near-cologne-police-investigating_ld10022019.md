_ehj/jsc._ Following a disruption at a substation in Bergheim near Cologne, police assume an intentional act, German media and the German Press Agency reported. A spokesman for the Essen police department said upon inquiry by the news agency that they did not assume a technical defect but rather an intentional act. He added that the background of the act was still unclear, and that investigations were underway into «anti-constitutional sabotage.» This refers to targeted acts of sabotage on critical public infrastructure that pursue an anti-state purpose.

The substation connects different voltages. Several lines failed due to a short circuit; however, current findings indicated no general power outage. According to the reports, police were called to the substation in Bergheim's Rheidt district on Tuesday evening around 8 p.m. due to a technical malfunction.

Herbert Reul, the Interior Minister for the western German state of North Rhine-Westphalia, said that unidentified individuals had placed cables over the overhead line, causing a short circuit. «Anyone who attacks that is attacking our country,» he said.

Earlier on Tuesday morning, unidentified individuals attempted to cause short circuits on high-voltage lines at a substation in Brandenburg. According to the authorities, this succeeded in two cases. Police are investigating the incident as sabotage. In addition to these recent cases, further acts of sabotage on power infrastructure occurred in recent months. In January, left-wing extremists damaged high-voltage lines near a power plant in southwestern Berlin.
