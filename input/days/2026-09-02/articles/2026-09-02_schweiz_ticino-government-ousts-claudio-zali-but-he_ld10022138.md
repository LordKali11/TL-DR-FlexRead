# The Ticino government ousts Claudio Zali – but he refuses to resign

*Lega cantonal councillor Claudio Zali: still in office, department leadership removed; new criminal investigation for violating sexual integrity*

The government crisis in Ticino, which has been simmering since the summer, reached a new peak. The prosecutor's office initiated a second criminal investigation against Lega cantonal councillor Claudio Zali, who is set to resign at the end of September due to several scandals. The investigation involves possible offenses against sexual integrity.

His four government colleagues reacted unusually strongly: they immediately stripped Zali of his authority over his Department of Environment and Transport. The deputy head of department and health director, Raffaele De Rosa (Centre), was appointed to temporarily lead it.

Ticino president of the cantonal government Marina Carobbio (SP) stated at a press conference on Wednesday that the government had already asked Zali the day before to immediately finalize his resignation, which was announced for the end of September. This move aimed to protect the credibility of the cantonal institutions. Because Zali refused, the collegium stripped him of departmental leadership on Wednesday.

The collegium itself cannot legally remove someone from office. Only the cantonal parliament can do that, and only in the event of a court judgment for a serious offense. Zali therefore remains a cantonal councillor until Sept. 30. According to State Chancellor Arnoldo Coduri, he may continue to attend meetings and use his office, but has only limited access to his email inbox, as he no longer bears responsibility for his department's files.

In early October, SVP National Council member Piero Marchesi, the first successor on the corresponding list, is set to become a member of the cantonal government in Zali's place. However, Marchesi declared himself ready to take office immediately if necessary. He will replace Zali on an interim basis and serve until the cantonal elections in spring 2027.

## Events dating back several years

The new investigation against Zali, who himself served for a long time as a judge at the cantonal criminal court, was made public by Italian-Swiss Radio and Television (RSI). RSI had obtained access to a communication from the Attorney General to the Presidency of the Grand Council. The Ticino prosecutor's office confirmed to the newspaper «La Regione» that proceedings for offenses against sexual integrity had been opened based on the statements of a third person. The accusations relate to events several years in the past.

According to information from the newspaper, the allegations involve Articles 191 and 193 of the Criminal Code, which deal with sexual acts with a person incapable of judgment or resistance, or by exploiting a relationship of dependency. Zali is presumed innocent.

The 64-year-old was not present at the press conference. Shortly before, he communicated in writing that the new proceedings had been initiated due to a false report by the 52-year-old woman who was found in Lake Lugano in mid-August and who had previously published secretly recorded phone calls. The prosecutor's office had already questioned him personally.

He said the woman had not reported offenses to her own detriment, but to the detriment of a third person. This person, however, had rejected the suspicions and denied that Zali had behaved improperly toward her. Zali therefore expected that the proceedings, which he considered unfounded, would soon be dropped. He emphasized that he would remain in office until the date he himself set.

## Questioned as a witness before her death

The 52-year-old woman, who has since died, published phone recordings from 2022 and 2023 in July – with a man who is said to be Zali. In the recordings, the man spoke with her about a third woman who had harassed the 52-year-old. According to RSI, the man urged his interlocutor to beat up the woman. The man also stated that he himself had already done so when the stalker appeared at his home. It remains unclear whether the incident actually occurred.

According to information from «La Regione,» the 52-year-old is the third person whose statements at least partially underpin the new investigation against Zali. She was questioned as a witness by the cantonal police and the prosecutor's office before her death.

The circumstances of the woman's death are still being investigated. Preliminary autopsy results showed no signs of external violence. Toxicological and histological findings are still pending. The prosecutor's office is not establishing a direct link between her death and the criminal proceedings against Zali.

Criminal proceedings had already been opened against Zali before. The earlier proceedings involve a meeting in his office in early June: the meeting involved a 14-year-old youth who had been temporarily placed in pre-trial detention in the adult prison La Farera near Lugano. Zali is said to have urgently questioned high-ranking officials and the responsible juvenile judge about the detention conditions and inquired about alternatives – apparently at the request of the boy's mother, an acquaintance of his. The prosecutor's office is investigating this case for abuse of office and attempted coercion.
