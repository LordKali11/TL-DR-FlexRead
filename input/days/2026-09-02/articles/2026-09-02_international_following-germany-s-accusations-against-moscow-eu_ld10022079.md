# Following Germany's accusations against Moscow: The EU is preparing new sanctions – Switzerland was also at the table for the first time

*Germany blames Russia for the drone incident in Leipzig and receives considerable support from EU member states. Ukraine wants more than just words.*

At the meeting of EU foreign ministers in Ireland, one word was heard repeatedly: solidarity. Practically all participants spontaneously pledged support to the German government. On Tuesday evening, it named Russia as the perpetrator of the attempted bomb attack on Leipzig Airport. While German opposition parties criticized the government's communication as expected, no European minister broke ranks in Wicklow, Ireland.

The unambiguous message directed at the Kremlin was that the EU states would not be intimidated by its hybrid warfare. «For Russia, it is a cheap way to fuel tensions in our society. But these activities will not deter us from supporting Ukraine and maintaining our pressure on Russia,» said Lithuanian Foreign Minister Kestutis Budrys, speaking on behalf of others.

The only question is how: Hundreds of billions of euros have been invested by EU states in Ukraine since 2022 in the form of weapons and financial aid, and 21 sanctions packages have been imposed against Russia. Without this support, Ukraine might no longer exist in its current form. At the same time, the pressure was clearly not enough to deter Vladimir Putin's regime from its campaign.

## Ukraine's Patriot missile needs

As the most immediate reaction to Germany's accusation, various European states, in agreement with the Chancellery, summoned the Russian ambassadors. EU High Representative Kaja Kallas announced punitive measures against 1,600 additional individuals associated with the Russian army, and the foreign ministers also discussed further sanctions against Russia's shadow fleet.

Even Russia's state assets frozen in Belgium are back on the table. Last autumn, talks had progressed to make 90 billion euros of these accessible to Ukraine. Due to rule-of-law concerns, the EU states ultimately refrained from this and instead granted a debt-financed loan of 90 billion euros. A handful of countries have now relaunched the debate – with an open outcome. Since it was an informal meeting, no decisions were made in Ireland.

However, Ukraine would prefer more armaments – especially Patriot air defense missiles given the approaching winter – to protect its energy infrastructure. Kyiv wants at least 300 units. However, Ukrainian Foreign Minister Andri Sibiha, who was also present in Ireland, left without firm commitments.

## Cassis misses Federal Council meeting

The Russian government spoke of a «serious mistake» that the German government had made with the accusation. Given the impending election defeat in Saxony-Anhalt, the German government had acted tactically, President Putin said. When asked about this, German Foreign Minister Johann Wadephul said the opposite was true – for electoral tactical reasons, he added, they probably would have been better off avoiding the topic, because it was now leading to «many discussions.»

Rather, Wadephul said he was pleased about the unity and support he felt from his ministerial colleagues – «beyond the circle of EU states, by the way,» he added. He meant, among others, Switzerland, which, according to the Federal Department of Foreign Affairs (FDFA), had been invited to an informal meeting of EU foreign ministers for the first time. Because Ignazio Cassis had to attend the Federal Council meeting, State Secretary Alexandre Fasel traveled to Ireland. Whether Switzerland would also apply renewed EU sanctions remains open. The Federal Council examines each individual case, but so far, with few exceptions, it has always adopted the punitive measures – most recently on Aug. 19.
