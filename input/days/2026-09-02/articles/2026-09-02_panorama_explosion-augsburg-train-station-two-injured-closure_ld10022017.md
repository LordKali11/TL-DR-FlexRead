# Explosion at Augsburg train station – two injured, closure lifted

*An explosion occurred at Augsburg train station early this morning. Police are on the scene with a large-scale operation. Train services were temporarily suspended.*

_ehj./jsc._ A large-scale police operation was underway at Augsburg main station since shortly after 5 a.m. The reason was an explosion in a building passageway. According to the German Press Agency, two people were slightly injured. They reportedly suffered acoustic trauma.

According to initial information, an unknown object exploded in a building passageway opposite the station around 3:15 a.m. The injured included a 17-year-old and an 18-year-old. According to police, both were Ukrainians. Window panes were also damaged by the explosion, police said. Investigators assumed they were coincidentally in the area as passers-by.

After the discovery of another suspicious object, police completely cordoned off the main station at 8:30 a.m. and suspended train services. The German Press Agency, citing police, reported that the object proved harmless after police investigations, and the station closure was lifted after about an hour and a half.

The police operation also affected road traffic and public transport. At 10:15 a.m., police announced that train services had resumed.
