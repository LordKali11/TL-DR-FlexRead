# In front of club legend Nuñez: Grasshoppers celebrate their first win of the season against St. Gallen

_(sda)_ The Grasshoppers gave themselves the best possible gift for the club's 140th anniversary. Coach Peter Zeidler's team celebrated a 2-0 victory against FC St. Gallen at their home ground, Letzigrund, securing their long-awaited first win of the season.

For the Eastern Swiss team, however, the negative spiral continues: The team suffered its fourth consecutive defeat across all competitions.

In front of emotionally celebrated GC legend Richard Nuñez, the Zurich team showed great commitment from the start. In the 36th minute, the Hoppers rewarded themselves: After a corner kick, the ball landed with captain Amir Abrashi, who scored the 1-0 goal like a true striker. For the veteran, who recently ended his 11-year goal drought in the Super League, it was already his second goal of the season.

St. Gallen tried hard but sinned in front of goal. Shortly before halftime, Christian Witzig missed the equalizer when he hit the post (43rd minute). The agile Aliou Baldé also failed multiple times against the GC defense or against the strong-performing league debutant Marvin Hübel in the Zurich goal.

In the second half, the hosts sealed the deal. Young-jun Lee, the South Korean who had only just been substituted in, scored the final 2-0 goal from a tight angle in the 69th minute.

## FC Luzern's added-time victory

FC Luzern also celebrated a home victory. In Central Switzerland, everything long pointed to a draw – until substitute Nando Toggenburger pushed the ball over the line from a scramble, securing a 2-1 victory for FCL. It was the final highlight of a match where numerous chances were missed by both sides.

Just like on Sunday, when Vaduz secured their first victory in the current championship, Lufti Dalipi was also involved in the dangerous actions of the Liechtenstein team on Wednesday evening. After a rather meager first half, the Young Boys loan player initiated the then-flattering lead for the visitors with a beautiful solo effort. Julian Stark only had to tap the ball in – 26 seconds after the restart.

The Luzern team rewarded themselves for their efforts before the hour mark. Right-back Ruben Dantas Fernandes scored a spectacular goal with a curling shot. Finally, just five minutes after the equalizer, Luzern celebrated what they thought was the 2-1 goal. However, because Toggenburger's goal was preceded by a foul, the goal did not count. Nevertheless, the Luzern joker was able to celebrate in the end. Like Ruben Dantas Fernandes, he scored for the first time in the Super League.

And so, Vaduz's losing streak against Luzern continues. The Liechtenstein team's last victory against the Central Swiss dates back to September 2008.

## Three disallowed goals in Thun

FC Thun and FC Lausanne-Sport drew goalless in the third match of Wednesday evening. In an intense game, valid goals were ultimately missing.

The visitors had the better start and celebrated twice early on. However, both Enzo Molebe's supposed goal (5th minute) and an own goal by Thun's Simon Lengen (7th minute) were correctly disallowed by the referee. Lausanne then dangerously tested goalkeeper Steffen again before the Bernese team increasingly took control.

After the break, Thun pushed for the lead. In the 63rd minute, Brighton Labeau made the home fans cheer, but the video referee intervened: The striker had carried the ball with his hand. In the final phase, Lausanne passionately defended against the attacking Thun team. Since the Oberland team could no longer break through the defensive wall, despite the fresh forces of Fabio Saiz and Nils Reichmuth, the fair 0-0 score remained.

Both teams thus miss the hoped-for jump into the upper half of the table.
