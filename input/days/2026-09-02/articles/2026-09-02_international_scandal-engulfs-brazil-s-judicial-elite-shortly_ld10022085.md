# Scandal engulfs Brazil's judicial elite shortly before presidential election

*New investigative files implicate powerful judges in the country and the Attorney General. Parts of the judiciary are now attempting to halt the investigations against their own ranks.*

**Alexander Busch** · International · 2026-09-02 · 975 words · [ld.10022085](https://www.nzz.ch/international/kurz-vor-der-praesidentschaftswahl-holt-ein-skandal-brasiliens-justizelite-ein-ld.10022085)

It has been known since late last year that Supreme Court justices are involved in the Banco Master scandal. This is the biggest corruption scandal in Brazil's banking sector in years. Numerous influential politicians, judges and bureaucrats are implicated. However, the Supreme Court repeatedly managed to let investigations against its own members come to nothing.

That could now be over: On Tuesday, Supreme Court Justice André Mendonça lifted the secrecy surrounding an extensive investigative report by the Federal Police. It contains detailed transcripts of messages that his fellow Justice Alexandre de Moraes and the accused banker Daniel Vorcaro had exchanged. Messages documenting Vorcaro's connections to Attorney General Paulo Gonet were also published.

## Arrest shortly before fleeing to Malta

It became clear that the banker had exchanged messages with Moraes several times a day in the days leading up to his arrest on Nov. 17, 2023. Vorcaro wanted to know if he should flee the country, if an arrest could still somehow be avoided, and if Moraes could prevent the liquidation of his bank at the central bank, he said. Vorcaro was eventually arrested as he attempted to fly to Malta on his private jet. Since then, he has been in pre-trial detention intermittently.

Vorcaro had transformed an insignificant financial service provider from the provinces into a bank with assets equivalent to 16 billion dollars in 10 years. He paid exorbitant interest rates on issued bonds, invested the capital raised in risky financial instruments, and hoped until the very end that one of the state-owned banks would take over his ailing portfolio. But eventually, the business model collapsed – despite extensive bribe payments at all levels. In mid-November 2025, Banco Master was placed under state administration, and its owner was arrested shortly thereafter.

## Published messages also politically sensitive

The top judiciary reacted as it had before when a scandal affected one of its members: Attorney General Gonet immediately tried to have the transcripts declared invalid. He argued that the Federal Police were not authorized to conduct investigations against members of the Supreme Court, and that only the plenary of the Supreme Court itself could decide on such matters. It also became known that Gonet had allowed himself and family members to be invited by Vorcaro on luxury trips abroad.

The Supreme Court thus once again stands at the center of a credibility debate. «Brazil faces one of the most serious moments in its institutional history,» says Bruno Brandão of Transparency International in Brazil. «Never before has the Federal Supreme Court been confronted with so many indications of having acted corruptly, accepted conflicts of interest, and allowed itself to be co-opted by private interests,» Brandão adds.

Justice Alexandre de Moraes, in particular, has caused enormous damage to the institution with his conduct. Renowned daily newspapers like «Folha de São Paulo» are demanding his immediate resignation. He is the same justice who led proceedings against former President Jair Bolsonaro last year, among other things, for an attempted coup.

However, while that proceeding was heavily criticized primarily by Bolsonaro's and Donald Trump's closest supporters, it is now his financial connections in the Banco Master scandal that are severely damaging Moraes's reputation.

## Moraes's wife and children also involved

Already during investigations late last year, it came to light that the law firm where Moraes's wife and children work was supposed to legally represent Banco Master for the equivalent of 24 million dollars over three years, according to the investigation. Surveys of other law firms showed that the fee was far above the usual. It is now public that the banker Vorcaro had promised the firm an additional 10 million dollars for usage shares in helicopters and private jets.

Moraes has so far denied any personal connection to the business relationship between Banco Master and his wife's law firm. However, the latest revelations show that Moraes personally made changes to the contract. What he answered the banker's repeated inquiries is not yet known. It is proven, however, that the justice personally met with the accused six times and accompanied him on two of his luxury trips.

How the scandal will affect the presidential election taking place in one month remains open: President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva leads Flávio Bolsonaro, the son of the incarcerated former president, in the polls.

While Lula has not yet been accused of any direct connections to Vorcaro, the situation is different for Flávio Bolsonaro. The banker reportedly gave him around 12 million dollars, allegedly to film a movie about his father. Flávio Bolsonaro had previously always denied even knowing the banker.

Nevertheless, the now highly publicized scandal could also damage Lula's popularity. Since Bolsonaro's conviction, Justice Moraes has been considered by the right-wing opposition as one of their greatest adversaries and is politically attributed by them to Lula's camp. Furthermore, many voters continue to associate Lula with major corruption scandals in the past.

This includes, above all, the corruption scandal surrounding the state oil company Petrobras, which heavily burdened the Workers' Party (PT) governments and also contributed to the political decline of Lula's successor, Dilma Rousseff. Lula and the PT have always rejected political responsibility for the corruption system that grew over the years.

For Lula, the scandal comes at a particularly bad time because he wanted to pass laws through Congress four weeks before the first round of elections that, he said, would allow him to run for a fourth term. Now, opposition lawmakers are blocking the legislature with their demands for the removal of Justice Moraes. 54 impeachment motions against the justice have now been filed.

The crucial factor now will be how the Supreme Court plenary deals with the Federal Police's new findings. Justice Mendonça has already requested that the plenary discuss the allegations. It remains to be seen whether the Senate President will give in to growing public pressure and initiate impeachment proceedings against Justice Moraes.
