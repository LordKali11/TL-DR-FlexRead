# Red-Green wants to force private property owners to build affordable housing: Social Democrats make strange retreat in city parliament

*Because vacancies weaken the left-wing coalition, the largest party in the city of Zurich suffers a tactical defeat in the council.*

**Michael von Ledebur** · Zürich · 2026-09-02 · 749 words · [ld.10021916](https://www.nzz.ch/zuerich/guenstige-wohnungen-in-der-stadt-zuerich-rot-gruen-macht-seltsamen-rueckzieher-ld.10021916)

The left-wing parties' demand is significant. It aims to pressure property owners in the City of Zurich. To do so, the Social Democrats and the Greens chose the sharpest weapon in parliamentary operations: a motion directly instructing the city government to prepare a proposal.

The red-green bloc is targeting «profit-oriented private entities» in the housing market. These entities, the red-green bloc says, are to be forced to create affordable housing – within the scope of all housing construction projects in which the city is involved in some form. The city is very often involved as a supervisory authority.

In all these cases, the red-green bloc says, the city should exert pressure on private entities. This goes significantly beyond the principle the city only recently introduced: that private developers must build a minimum proportion of affordable housing if they are granted higher utilization when building land is rezoned or redeveloped.

From now on, a minimum proportion is also to apply even if private entities do not gain any additional utilization, the bloc argues. And the city's role as a supervisory and approval authority, they add, is to serve as leverage.

It is an idea that alarms the bourgeois parties. This approach, the bourgeois parties say, will definitively deter private entities, with whom the city has not been gentle recently anyway. This is despite the fact that private entities build by far the most housing in the City of Zurich, and the vacancy rate is record-low, they add.

On Wednesday, the motion by the Social Democrats and the Greens was on the agenda in the city parliament. SP City Councilor Anjushka Früh began her speech with expected words: «There is a huge housing shortage in Zurich. That is why it is important for the city to buy properties.» However, the city must also «be in contact» with private players and try to assert its influence and «demand consideration,» she said.

What followed was surprising. Früh said that «a lot is already happening» in this area. And because that is the case, the motion is being withdrawn, she added. This is a process rarely seen in the city parliament.

One could conclude that City of Zurich politics has sufficiently addressed the housing issue. In the last five years, the three left-wing parties – the Social Democrats, the Greens, and the Alternative List – submitted no fewer than 50 motions on the subject, demanding, for example, stricter rent controls or remedies against empty terminations.

In addition, the Social Democrats have occupied the Building Department for many years anyway – so the left-wing city parliamentarians could simply call the city hall directly.

But, of course, such insights probably did not prompt SP city parliamentarian Früh to withdraw. Rather, the city's largest party was obviously forced to resort to political maneuvering.

The party strategists on the left must have realized at the beginning of the council debate that it would not be enough for a majority, they deduced. The three left-wing parties in the city parliament have only a 63 to 62 majority when all parliamentarians are present. However, on this evening, the left side had too many vacancies.

The left-wing parties therefore tried everything to prevent their motion from being debated.

First, in the preceding discussion about a women's quota for leadership positions in the city administration, several speakers prolonged the debate with numerous additional contributions. And then Council President Ivo Bieri (SP) declared the session closed: There was no longer enough time to deal with the housing motion, he said.

However, Sven Sobernheim (GLP) did not let this pass. It was obvious that the left side had deliberately debated at length and thus «filibustered» – because they knew they would be defeated, Sobernheim contended. Sobernheim forced the continuation of the discussion by means of a procedural motion, the center-right prevailed, and the debate continued. Afterward, Früh withdrew her motion.

FDP parliamentary group leader Emanuel Tschannen said after the council debate: «If we on the bourgeois side are well-represented and work together, we can win votes.» The red-green bloc is outnumbered partly due to a vacancy among the Greens: a member resigned there, and the succession is delayed.

However, several parliamentarians from the Green Liberal Party (GLP) and bourgeois parties are convinced that the Social Democrats will re-submit the motion in a slightly altered form, they believe. Früh was not available for comment. The idea was «at least delayed,» said FDP parliamentary group leader Tschannen. It would take months until the matter came before the council again in a possible new edition, he added.
