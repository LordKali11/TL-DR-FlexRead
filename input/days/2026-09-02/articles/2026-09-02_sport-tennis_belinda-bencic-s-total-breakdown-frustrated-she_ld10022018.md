# Belinda Bencic's «total breakdown»: Frustrated, she hits a ball into the crowd, then is eliminated

*Bencic avoids disqualification but loses to Julia Putintseva in the US Open first round. Her elimination means the last Swiss representative in the main draw is out.*

Little went right for Belinda Bencic in her first-round match at the US Open. Even a ball she blindly hit backward out of frustration went awry, and could have led to her disqualification.

Her opponent, Kazakhstan's Julia Putintseva, immediately and loudly complained, demanding the Swiss player's expulsion. Six years ago, Novak Djokovic was also disqualified at the US Open for a similar outburst. However, the Serb hit a line judge in the neck.

The chair umpire at Bencic's match also had experience with frustration shots: one such shot had hit Arnaud Gabas in the eye in 2017. He, however, issued only a warning to Bencic. The reportedly hit spectator indicated that «everything was fine.» Bencic apologized immediately.

In the end, however, that did not help Bencic either. After almost three hours, the world No. 12 lost to Putintseva, No. 84, in three sets with scores of 6-4, 5-7, 3-6.

## First the rain, then the frustration shot

After Viktorija Golubic, Simona Waltert, and Stan Wawrinka, Bencic's elimination means the last Swiss representative in the main draw at the US Open is out in the first round. As in 2020 and 2024, no Swiss player reached the second round.

The omens were already bad for Bencic: her match could only start after a delay of almost four hours due to rain. Nevertheless, she started better, broke early, but then conceded the rebreak to make it 3-3. After that, Bencic secured the first set.

The match was hotly contested on both sides. After the double fault with which Putintseva handed Bencic the first set, her racket suffered. The Kazakh player had generally caused a stir on several occasions. This year, she celebrated a victory at the Australian Open with a provocative dance; two years ago, she demonstratively ignored a ball girl and let tossed balls bounce off her. Fans interpreted it as disrespect.

In the second set, Bencic's frustration at missing the 3-0 lead on her serve and immediately conceding the rebreak led to her outburst. «That was 100% my mistake. I absolutely did not want that,» Bencic said afterward in an interview with SRF, adding that she had wanted to shoot against the band.

## Another setback at a major tournament

After the match, Bencic said that she had not felt provoked by her opponent. However, she did not leave the demands for disqualification uncommented: «Maybe she should have focused on herself,» Bencic said. Putintseva, she added, could complain less about such things given her racket throws.

After the incident, Bencic's rhythm was definitively broken. Putintseva managed a break at 5-5 and won the set 5-7. In the third set, Bencic snarled several times towards her own box, demanding more support. Putintseva grew stronger and played more calmly. After a 3-2 lead, Bencic did not win a single game more. She called it a «total breakdown» after the match.

Overall, the balance sheet for this year's major tournaments was meager: two Round of 16 appearances at Roland Garros and Wimbledon, a second-round exit in Melbourne, and now an early first-round exit in New York. For the first time since Roland Garros in 2023, Bencic did not make it past the opening round at a major tournament.

Bencic said she had felt physically well and that the hip problems that recently led to a retirement and a forfeit at the last tournaments in Toronto and Cincinnati had healed. She therefore seemed perplexed after the defeat. «It is certainly one that hurts more,» Bencic said.
