Benjamin Netanyahu has become lonely recently. Political companions have turned away from him, and he has lost support among his core voters and within his own party. But one person, at least, can always be relied upon: his wife Sara.

In an interview aired on Monday, the wife of the Israeli Prime Minister made outrageous allegations, insinuating that opposition politician Yair Golan had known about the Hamas massacre on Oct. 7, 2023, in advance. Evidence? She had none.

Instead, what she presented as evidence should have actually spoken for him. The leader of the left-wing Democrats and former deputy army chief had driven to the site of the Nova music festival on the morning of the attack and saved several lives there.

## Golan's defamation claim against Sara Netanyahu

Sara Netanyahu now interpreted this precisely against him, suggesting it was suspicious that such a high-ranking military official had been there early in the morning, at a time when others, such as the Prime Minister, her husband, had known nothing about it.

Golan did not tolerate this, calling her statement defamation. He had a lawyer's letter delivered to Sara Netanyahu. She should retract the statement, apologize, and donate to the survivors of the festival, the letter demanded. If she does not comply, he intends to sue for the equivalent of around 670,000 Swiss francs.

Sara Netanyahu, however, is not considering an apology. She claims Golan spreads wild lies and distorts her words, and demanded he retract his accusations within 48 hours and donate to a military academy. Otherwise, she said, she would sue him in turn for the equivalent of around 270,000 Swiss francs.

In the country, Sara Netanyahu's insinuations are primarily perceived as a political plot. The left-wing opposition politician received support from deep within the right-wing camp. Former Prime Minister Naftali Bennett accused the Netanyahu family and their entourage of spreading a sick, false, and dangerous conspiracy against the army, intelligence agencies, and Golan.

Gadi Eisenkot, former army chief and currently Netanyahu's main challenger, called the treason theory outrageous and without any factual basis. He said it served only to incite hatred and keep the Netanyahu government in office, adding that the Prime Minister was evading his responsibility for the worst failure in Israel's history.

## Netanyahu's obstruction of investigation

In fact, the reckoning with this failure is far from complete. Almost three years after the massacre, there is no independent investigation into the errors that preceded the attack.

This is primarily due to Netanyahu himself. He rejects a state investigative commission whose members are to be appointed by the Supreme Court. Instead, his coalition launched a body whose mandate the government itself determines. Netanyahu would thus have a say in the investigation of his own failure.

That Sara Netanyahu's statement was not a thoughtless slip of the tongue, but part of a calculated election campaign by her husband, is suggested by a report from the public broadcaster Kan on Tuesday evening. The goal of the action was to spread disinformation and absolve Netanyahu at all costs of his responsibility for the Oct. 7 massacre, the broadcaster reported, citing anonymous sources in the Likud camp.

The campaign is led by the family but has close ties to the party leadership, Likud sources say. For example, Sara Netanyahu was accompanied to the TV studio by Topaz Luk, a close advisor to the Prime Minister. The Likud also demanded and received the unedited raw footage before the edited version aired. These sources add that the Prime Minister himself is also involved in the matter.

## Son's accusation of «treason» against the army

His most recent appearance also suggests this. In an Instagram Live broadcast on Tuesday, Netanyahu claimed his security chiefs had deliberately let him sleep on the morning of the attack, asserting they had known that he would otherwise have alerted the entire army and prevented the attack.

It is a claim that Ronen Bar, then head of the domestic intelligence agency, had already denied under oath in April 2025. On Tuesday evening, Netanyahu half-heartedly backtracked. He had not spoken of treason, but of failure, he said.

Sara Netanyahu, however, is not the first in the family to circulate conspiracy theories about Oct. 7. As early as 2024, his son Yair hinted there had been a plot within the army leadership before the attack. His father had to contain the matter at the time. Before lawmakers, he said there had been «a serious intelligence failure,» but «no treason.»

Whether the family campaign is effective, however, remains an open question. The Israelis, at least, are not convinced by the Prime Minister's assurances. In recent polls, a majority has repeatedly expressed support for Netanyahu taking responsibility for Oct. 7 and resigning.
