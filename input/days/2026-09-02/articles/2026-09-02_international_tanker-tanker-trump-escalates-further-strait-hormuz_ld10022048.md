The U.S. succeeded in establishing a sea route in the Strait of Hormuz, running through Omani coastal waters and independent of Iran. However, Tehran does not want to lose control of the strait at any price. The Revolutionary Guards continue to sporadically hit oil tankers from the Arab Gulf states. For this reason, U.S. President Donald Trump decided to retaliate in kind: For the first time, the U.S. attacked two Iranian tankers on Tuesday.

Washington’s new rule of engagement in the Strait of Hormuz is «tanker for tanker,» a government official told the news portal Axios. The White House hopes this will deter Iran from further attacks on cargo ships passing through the strait without Iranian permission.

## Rejection of diplomacy

In addition to the tankers, the U.S. military attacked other targets along the Iranian coast in the Persian Gulf on Tuesday. Air defense and radar systems, as well as military equipment for laying mines and communications, were hit, Central Command in the Middle East explained on Wednesday.

With the operation, Washington wants to prevent the Iranian armed forces from rebuilding the infrastructure on the coast, which was destroyed in the previous war, to attack ships in the strait. These attacks reduced the risk of Iranian attacks on ships in the Strait of Hormuz for approximately one month, sources in U.S. government circles say.

It is possible that not everything went according to plan during the wave of attacks. According to Iranian media reports, U.S. missiles reportedly also hit a wedding party in a residential area near the coastal city of Kuhestak. Reportedly, five people were killed and more than 50 others injured. A U.S. military spokesperson stated that the military was aware of the reports in the Iranian state media, but the U.S. never targets civilians. Independently verifying the incident is difficult to date. Already on the first day of the war in February, a U.S. projectile struck an Iranian school. More than 150 people, mostly children, died.

On Truth Social, Donald Trump warned the Tehran regime on Tuesday against retaliating: «Should Iran’s failed nation retaliate for this justified attack, it will be hit much harder,» the U.S. president wrote. «When it’s over, very little will be left of the Islamic Republic.» On Wednesday, Trump followed up. He was now in a much better position and no longer wanted to force Tehran to the negotiating table, Trump confidently stated on his microblogging service. The U.S. had almost total control over the Strait of Hormuz, and the Iranian economy was collapsing. He again called for the overthrow of the regime: «When will the Iranian people finally rise up and fight?»

## Mine explosion reported by Iran

However, Tehran remains unimpressed to date. Its armed forces immediately responded with retaliatory attacks this time as well. They attacked U.S. military bases and other targets with ballistic missiles and drones in Jordan, Iraq, Kuwait, and Bahrain. The Jordanian military reported that it had intercepted 10 ballistic missiles, and three had struck uninhabited areas.

According to the Iranian Revolutionary Guards, two oil tankers struck mines in the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday, and their crews were evacuated by the U.S. military. Should this be confirmed, it would refute Trump’s assertions that the strait was now free of sea mines.

Trump hopes to force the regime into concessions or even bring about its collapse with the indefinite naval blockade against Iran. As Reuters reports, Tehran has been unable to export its oil by sea for seven weeks. Iran can currently only supply crude oil to China that is already on tankers in Asia, Reuters writes. The economic hardship in Iran is therefore likely to worsen further. Inflation for food already stood at 130% in July. Because the country also needs to import part of its gasoline needs, fuel is becoming scarce at gas stations.

At the same time, the U.S. has not yet brought the Strait of Hormuz under its control. For ships transporting oil from Arab Gulf states through the Strait of Hormuz at night under U.S. protection, the passage remains risky. On Wednesday, oil prices rose again above $90 per barrel. With an eye on the midterm elections in November in the U.S., this is not good news for Trump and his Republican Party. Due to the unpopular Iran war, his approval ratings fell to a new low. Only one in three Americans is satisfied with Trump’s governance.
