_(sda)_ Lugano accompanies FC Thun into the Conference League group stage. While the Swiss champions' participation had already been confirmed as a consolation prize a week ago after their 0-7 defeat to Lech Poznan in the first leg of the Europa League play-offs, Lugano needed an impressive feat from Dereck Moncada while playing short-handed to advance.

Lugano came under severe pressure in Batumi, Georgia, where Maccabi was temporarily based. The Swiss representative played with one player fewer from the 58th minute. Without Mattia Zanotti, who had received two yellow cards, Lugano, which had been quite dominant until then, came under pressure and conceded a penalty goal in the 69th minute, falling behind 0-1.

Trailing, Mattia Croci-Torti’s team had to focus almost entirely on defending – until the 79th minute. It was then that Honduran striker Dereck Moncada had his decisive moment. The 18-year-old, who came on in the 60th minute, won the ball just before the midfield line and ran solo almost to the edge of the penalty area, where he shot magnificently into the far upper corner of the goal.

Lugano will play in its fifth group stage since 2017. Thun is in this lucrative European Cup phase for the first time since 2014. After suffering a thrashing a week ago in the home game against Polish champions Lech Poznan, they had nothing left to play for and drew 2-2. Lugano and Thun will learn their six Conference League opponents in a draw early Friday afternoon.

## St. Gallen and Sion eliminated

FC St. Gallen and FC Sion missed out on advancing. FC St. Gallen lost the second leg of the play-offs 2-3 against Nordsjaelland and ended their European Cup dreams after a 0-1 defeat in the first leg.

The decisive goal came ten minutes before the final whistle. Boe Rasmussen beat goalkeeper Lawrence Ati-Zigi and gave Nordsjaelland a 3-2 lead. Before that, St. Gallen, who played short-handed from the 40th minute, bravely defended against further goals from the visitors. They even had a chance to equalize the aggregate score against the Danes, who were anything but dominant in the second leg.

This defeat will likely annoy St. Gallen for a long time. The 2-4 aggregate score after both legs prevented the Eastern Swiss club from playing in the Conference League group stage in the fall.

FC Sion was eliminated by Ajax Amsterdam. After the 2-4 home defeat, their chances of advancing against the six-time European Cup winners were no longer very high. In the Johan Cruyff Arena, the Sion players put in a good attacking performance in their 3-5 loss, mainly thanks to hat-trick scorer Winsley Boteli. Defensively, however, they were far too vulnerable as the game progressed to achieve an upset.
