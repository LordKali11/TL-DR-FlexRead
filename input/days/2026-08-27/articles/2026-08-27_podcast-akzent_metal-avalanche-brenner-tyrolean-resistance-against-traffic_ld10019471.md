## In this podcast episode

At the end of May, more than 4,000 people marched on the Brenner highway in Tyrol. They paralyzed traffic. The «traffic jam of the century» some feared for the Whitsun weekend was prevented, but the participants of the demonstration certainly captured the attention of many.

Especially in Germany, the Brenner is a synonym for holidays in Italy for many. It is the last stage that must be completed, preferably without traffic jams or slow-moving traffic. But that is becoming increasingly difficult. «The numbers continue to rise,» explains Austria correspondent Meret Baumann. She was in the Wipptal, through which the A 13 runs, and says that the protest drew attention to the situation of the residents, but the so-called «traffic jam refugees» still come. The diversion traffic from the congested highway brings noise, hardly any paying customers, and severely impedes the people in Matrei or Gries am Brenner. Even ambulances often do not reach their destination.
