# These three municipalities in the Zurich area are most attractive for families

*A study shows where families with average incomes live best. The decisive factor is the cost of living. In Aarau, these are 28% lower than in Zurich.*

Families looking for a new home in the Zurich region with average incomes should look beyond the canton borders. The best ratio of location quality and cost of living is offered neither by Zurich, Uster nor Winterthur. Aarau ranks first, ahead of Baden and Schaffhausen. The city of Zurich comes in fourth.

This is the result of the latest UBS study on residential attractiveness in various regions of Switzerland. The bank examined which municipalities offer the most attractive overall package for a family with two children. It considered various criteria in the areas of infrastructure and accessibility, leisure and quality of life and costs.

## Zurich's advantages, but still falling short

For the comparison, UBS takes a four-person family with a gross income of 150,000 Swiss francs and assets of 80,000 Swiss francs, living in rented accommodation and requiring 110 square meters. A total of 38 variables are included in the calculation. They range from travel times to major centers, the density of shops, medical care, daycare centers and schools, to gastronomy, sports facilities, green spaces, aircraft noise and crime. For costs, UBS considers rents, taxes and health insurance premiums.

The example of Zurich shows how difficult it is to perform well everywhere. In terms of infrastructure and accessibility, the city receives top marks in the UBS assessment. The same applies to leisure and quality of life. In terms of costs, however, it falls into the lowest category. Life here is very expensive compared to other regions, which, the study notes, significantly reduces its overall attractiveness as a place to live.

Aarau achieves the most attractive combination. Aargau's cantonal capital also ranks highest for infrastructure and leisure, but the cost of living, unlike Zurich, is in the medium range. Baden is similarly strong in infrastructure and leisure but more expensive than Aarau.

Of course, this does not mean that Aarau or Baden offer the same as Zurich. Those who value a university, international concert halls, top-tier gastronomy or a particularly wide range of cultural offerings will find more of these in Zurich. For families, however, other factors often matter: easily accessible schools, childcare, shopping facilities, sports facilities and short distances. Especially in these everyday factors, medium-sized cities can also perform very well.

The decisive difference is made by the cost of living. According to UBS, costs are about 28% lower for the example four-person family in Aarau than in the city of Zurich. In Schaffhausen, they are even 34% lower.

## Thousands of francs difference, just a few train minutes away

Families do not even have to travel far from Zurich to save a lot of money.

A four-person average family living in a suburban municipality 10 to 15 minutes by public transport from Zurich spends, according to UBS calculations, an average of about 11,500 francs less per year on rent, health insurance premiums and taxes than in the city. From a public transport travel time of 45 minutes, the difference grows to more than 20,000 francs annually. This disparity is less pronounced in other major cities such as Bern, Basel, Geneva and Lausanne.

However, the savings are not entirely free. Those living further out and working in Zurich accept longer commutes, additional travel costs and, above all, more commuting time. UBS therefore also considers the accessibility of a municipality. The extent to which this disadvantage weighs depends not least on how often someone actually has to commute to work.

## Medium-sized cities: secret winners

UBS deliberately refrains from creating a ranking for all of Switzerland. This is because about 70% of all moves take place within a radius of less than ten kilometers. Most people therefore look for their place of residence within an existing living and working area. The bank therefore divided Switzerland into ten regions and compared municipalities only within these areas.

One of the central results of the study is that medium-sized cities often beat the major centers. In nine of these ten regions, a medium-sized city takes first. In Western Switzerland, Vevey beats Geneva and Lausanne, among others; in the Bern-Fribourg region, Fribourg and Murten are ahead of Bern. Only Basel can maintain its position as a major center at the top of its region.

## Medium-sized centers often more attractive for families

This is because medium-sized cities are large enough to offer good infrastructure and a wide range of schools, medical care, shopping facilities, culture and leisure. At the same time, housing and thus the cost of living is often significantly cheaper than in large cities.

Schaffhausen particularly succeeds in combining «good and affordable.» The city performs slightly worse than Aarau or Baden in terms of leisure and quality of life. However, it is still above average in this area. At the same time, the cost of living is below average. According to UBS, only four municipalities in Switzerland achieve this: Besides Schaffhausen, these are Altdorf, Estavayer and Appenzell.

## Different calculations for high earners

However, the «most attractive» place to live always exists only for a specific household. This applies even to the group of families examined here. If income changes, the ranking also changes.

For a family with a comparatively low gross income of 85,000 francs, Aarau remains in first in the Zurich-Aarau-Schaffhausen region. This is followed by Schaffhausen and Baden. Zurich does not even make it into the top ten. Instead, for example, Olten, Zofingen, Uster, Greifensee or Winterthur are mentioned.

With a gross annual income of 300,000 francs per family, the situation suddenly looks different. Now Freienbach ranks first for families, ahead of Lachen and Rapperswil-Jona. Zurich again takes fourth, Rüschlikon fifth, Zollikon sixth and Kilchberg seventh. Küsnacht also now makes it into the top ten.

Here, another factor becomes more significant: taxes. The higher the income, the more significant low taxes become – and the better high housing costs can be absorbed. For an average income, however, expensive housing offsets the tax advantage of many Lake Zurich municipalities. UBS therefore concludes that tax-advantaged places to live only pay off for many households at very high incomes.
