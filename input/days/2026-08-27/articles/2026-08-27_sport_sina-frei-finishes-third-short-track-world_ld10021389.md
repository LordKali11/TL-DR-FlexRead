# Sina Frei finishes third in short track world championship – gold for Austria's Stigger

*Sina Frei won the bronze medal in the short track at the Mountain Bike World Championships in Val di Sole. Austria's Laura Stigger secured gold. The Swiss men remained without a medal.*

_(sda)_ At the start of the tenth and final lap, Sina Frei set out to overtake the two competitors ahead of her on the outside before a right turn. But Sweden's Jenny Rissveds swung wide in the turn, which almost brought Frei to a standstill and dropped her back to the last position of the leading group of five. «It was rule-compliant, but certainly not fair in terms of sportsmanship. But I also have to be able to deal with that,» the 29-year-old said in an SRF interview.

Despite the incident, Frei did not give up, moving back into third place on the following climb. However, she could not secure more than bronze in the long final sprint. Austria's Stigger surged from position 4 all the way to the front, and Britain's Evie Richards finished second. «It just wasn't meant to be, but on Sunday I have another chance,» Frei said. The cross-country race will then take place in northern Italy.

## **Koller's annoying mistake**

Behind Rissveds, Nicole Koller finished fifth, not sprinting at all because she thought the race would continue for another lap. Defending champion Alessandra Keller, winner a year ago at the home world championships in Valais, reached the finish line in sixth place, 12 seconds back. A fourth Swiss rider, Ronja Blöchlinger, finished 11th.

There were numerous successes for Swiss juniors on Thursday. In the U23 category, Monique Halter won world championship gold in the short track, and an hour later, her younger brother, Nicolas Halter, also triumphed. In the U19 junior women's cross-country race, Anja Grossmann won the world championship, and Marwan Barhoumi won bronze in the junior men's race.
