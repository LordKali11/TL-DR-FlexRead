_(dpa) _In an act of violence at a school near Berlin on Thursday (Aug. 28), a woman and a man were killed, according to information from the German Press Agency. A suspect, a 19-year-old German man, was provisionally arrested. The victims died at a school in Gosen-Neu Zittau in eastern Brandenburg.

Police have since provided further details about the two deceased, as German media reported. One of the victims was reportedly a 30-year-old kindergarten teacher. The kindergarten is located on the same premises as the primary and secondary school. Previously, it had been assumed that the deceased 30-year-old was the caretaker, which police did not confirm.

The second victim is a young woman. She is said to be the suspect's ex-girlfriend. Police confirmed the connection. It was initially unclear whether the 18-year-old was a student.

Investigators did not assume there was still a danger at the school, according to the information. On Platform X, the Brandenburg police had previously written about an operation together with rescue services. A male person had been provisionally arrested who, according to current findings, was connected to the crime, police said. «We are currently securing the crime scene,» police said.

The crime occurred on a school campus run by a private operator, surrounded by forests and lakes on the outskirts of Gosen-Neu Zittau, a town with around 3,500 inhabitants. A Gymnasium, secondary school, and technical secondary school are located there. This model is intended to promote commonalities, and permeability between the individual school types is emphasized.
