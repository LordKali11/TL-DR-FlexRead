**What happened:** At least 359 people died in the devastating flash flood in the border region between Nepal and China. More than 1,300 are still missing, including eight Germans. Chinese authorities now fear further floods. According to Chinese state television, citing the Ministry of Water Resources, a reservoir formed at a river's mouth on the Nepalese side. There was a risk of a dam breach, sources said. To the latest developments

**The background:** The flash flood was possibly triggered by a glacier collapse. The fall of the ice masses from a great height could have transformed them into water. However, a glacial lake outburst is also possible. Several glacial lakes in the Himalayas require close monitoring because they are prone to breaching. The flash flood buried entire settlements over a stretch of more than 60 kilometers. For many residents, there was no escape. More on the background

**The Gyirong border crossing:** The flash flood also destroyed an important border crossing between China and Nepal. It is Beijing's strategic corridor through the Himalayas. A flash flood destroyed the bridge in 2025. Why do Nepal and China nevertheless continue to rely on this dangerous trade route? To the background

**What happened:** Former Bosnian Serb military leader Ratko Mladic died in The Hague at the age of 84. Mladic was sentenced to life imprisonment for war crimes and served his sentence in the international prison in The Hague. To the report

**A look back:** Mladic had, among other things, ordered the massacre of more than 8,000 people – mainly men and young men – in the eastern Bosnian enclave of Srebrenica in July 1995. In 2017, he was convicted by the Hague War Crimes Tribunal.
