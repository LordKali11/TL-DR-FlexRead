**⏱ 48 hours before the attack**

In a New Year's interview, Nicolás Maduro offered the US serious talks on an agreement to combat drug trafficking. The US could also invest in Venezuela's oil sector «when, where, and how they want.» The regime also released another 88 political prisoners. Ninety-nine were already released from prison at Christmas.

US President Donald Trump had deployed 15,000 American soldiers, the largest military force in the Caribbean in decades. He had said it would be wise for Maduro to step down voluntarily. He declared Venezuela a narco-state flooding the US with drugs. However, Venezuela is not so much considered a drug-producing country as a transit country – and moreover, more so a transit country to Europe than to the US.

**⏱ 24 hours before the attack**

Anticipating a violent attack by the US against him, the 63-year-old ruler Maduro has changed his Cuban bodyguards and his sleeping place every night, reports the «New York Times».

Meanwhile, the US Coast Guard is abandoning the search for survivors of the recent attacks on alleged drug boats in the Pacific. Five people are reported to have been killed in the attacks. The US military has attacked speedboats suspected of carrying drugs approximately 30 times since September 2025.

**⏱ January 3, 2026, 2:00 a.m. in Caracas**

Explosions can be heard in Venezuela's capital, Caracas; cell phone videos show fireballs and huge plumes of smoke. La Carlota, a military airport in the city center, and Fuerte Tiuna in southern Caracas, home to the Ministry of Defense and an air force base, are among the first targets of the American airstrikes. Power outages occur in the surrounding neighborhoods. Attacks are also reported on La Guaira, Venezuela's main port city, and two other states, Miranda and Aragua.

**⏱ 3:38 a.m**

The American broadcaster CBS has received confirmation from government officials in Washington: President Trump has ordered the attack on military installations and other targets in Venezuela. The military strike was reportedly planned for the Christmas holidays but was postponed due to adverse weather conditions and the airstrikes against Islamists in Nigeria, which Trump also ordered.

At the same time, the Venezuelan government issued a lengthy statement. It declared the serious military aggression a violation of the UN Charter and asserted that the US was only interested in Venezuela's natural resources. The population was called upon to resist, and President Maduro had declared a state of emergency.

**⏱ 3:52 a.m**

Cuban President Miguel Díaz-Canel called on X for a swift international condemnation of the «criminal attack by the US.» Cuba is considered Venezuela's most important military partner. Up to 30,000 Cuban security forces are believed to be in the country. Cuba is dependent on cheap oil from Venezuela. Will Trump now also attack the island that has long been the US's biggest adversary in the Western Hemisphere?

**⏱ 4:00 a.m**

Black helicopters fly across the night sky over Caracas, firing missiles. The US military has apparently quickly gained air superiority over the capital. The international airport is reportedly already under their control.

**⏱ 5:21 a.m**

President Trump announced Maduro's arrest on his platform TruthSocial: «The United States of America has successfully conducted a large-scale strike against Venezuela and its leader, President Nicolás Maduro, who, along with his wife, was captured and flown out of the country.» Trump announced a press conference for 11:00 a.m. local time at his private residence in Mar-a-Lago. It was later revealed that the elite US Army unit Delta Force had carried out the operation against Maduro. The destination of the president and his wife was unknown at that time.

**⏱ 5:28 a.m**

In a video posted on social media, Defense Minister Vladimir Padrino López addressed the Venezuelan people, calling for united resistance and announcing the deployment of armed forces throughout the country, who he said would follow «Maduro's orders.» He made no mention of Maduro's arrest. «They have attacked us, but they will not subdue us,» he said. López warned against anarchy and unrest: «Let us not succumb to the panic that the enemy is trying to sow.» Earlier reports indicated that López's house in Caracas had been hit by airstrikes.

**⏱ 5:31 a.m**

Trump answered the phone after three rings, a New York Times journalist writes . The president sounded tired and said of the attack: «A lot of good planning and a lot of great, great soldiers and great people. It really was a brilliant operation.» The journalist wanted to know if the president had obtained congressional approval. «We'll discuss it,» Trump said. «We'll have a press conference.» When asked what he envisioned for Venezuela next, he replied: «You'll hear all about it at 11 a.m.» Then, fifty seconds later, he ended the call.

**⏱ 6:16 a.m**

The first US senators are reacting. Mike Lee, a Republican representing the state of Utah, writes on X that he just spoke with Secretary of State Marco Rubio and learned from him that Maduro is to be tried in the US. The military strike, he claims, was solely intended to facilitate Maduro's arrest by US officials. Such a step, Lee concludes, is likely constitutionally permissible. Andy Kim, a Democratic senator from New Jersey, on the other hand, accuses Rubio and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth of blatantly lying to the Senate. **⏱ 7:06 a.m**

The Russian Foreign Ministry condemns the US attacks and calls for dialogue.

**⏱ 8:00 a.m**

Spain has offered its services to help find a peaceful, negotiated solution for Venezuela, the Foreign Ministry announced in a statement . Earlier this morning, Kaja Kallas, the EU's High Representative for Foreign Affairs and Security Policy, appealed to the United States, without naming it directly. **⏱ 10:00 a.m**

Trump can hardly wait for his press conference. He gives Fox News a 26-minute telephone interview beforehand . Surrounded by generals, he watched the operation on screen at Mar-a-Lago: «I watched it like I was watching a TV show.» He can't stop raving about how brilliantly the operation went. The US soldiers were equipped with cutting torches to cut open steel gates if necessary. But Maduro was captured before he could escape to his bunker.

They had built a replica of the house where Maduro had been staying beforehand, so that special forces could train for the operation, Trump said. As far as he knew, there were no fatalities on the US side, but some injuries. Maduro and his wife were airlifted by helicopter. «A nice flight, I'm sure they enjoyed it.» The two are now on the USS Iwo Jima, en route to New York.

Trump repeatedly complains about the drug cartels and criminals from Venezuela. But then he also says that the US will be very heavily involved in the Venezuelan oil industry in the future. «We are prepared.»

US Attorney General Pam Bondi has released the 25-page indictment against Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and five other defendants, including his wife Cilia Flores and his son Nicolás Ernesto.
